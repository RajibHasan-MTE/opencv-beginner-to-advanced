# OpenCV Beginner to Advanced 🚀

Welcome to **OpenCV Beginner to Advanced** — a structured and practical journey into the world of computer vision. This repository is designed to help you learn OpenCV step-by-step through real practice code, starting from the basics and advancing toward complex, real-time projects.

---

## 📁 Repository Structure

opencv-beginner-to-advanced/ ├── beginner/ │ ├── image_read_display.py │ ├── drawing_shapes.py │ ├── video_capture.py │ ├── color_spaces.py │ └── README.md ├── intermediate/ │ ├── edge_detection.py │ ├── face_detection.py │ ├── image_blending.py │ ├── object_tracking.py │ └── README.md ├── advanced/ │ ├── face_recognition_realtime.py │ ├── motion_detection.py │ ├── ocr_with_tesseract.py │ ├── lane_detection.py │ └── README.md └── README.md


---

## 🧠 What You Will Learn

- ✅ Image and video handling
- ✅ Drawing and manipulating shapes on images
- ✅ Converting between color spaces
- ✅ Detecting edges and contours
- ✅ Face detection with Haar cascades and DNN
- ✅ Object tracking and motion detection
- ✅ Real-time face recognition
- ✅ OCR using Tesseract
- ✅ Lane detection (self-driving car simulation basics)
